Method & applicability

ECOB is a reduced-order screening framework: it preserves the principal hydraulic–economic optimum in a compact correlation rather than repeating diameter-by-diameter full-process optimization.

1 · Locate the optimum

Full-process hydraulic and economic calculations identify minimum-cost transport designs and map them into EVR space.

2 · Correlate ECOBc

The ECOB center is represented using mass flow and dominant techno-economic drivers, especially electricity and steel cost.

3 · Implement engineering size

The target ID is converted to commercial NPS and wall thickness, then used for rapid pressure-drop, pumping and cost screening.
