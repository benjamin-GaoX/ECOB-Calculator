ECOB 2.0 decision logic

The purpose of the tool is not merely to report two diameters. It translates the distance between the economic and environmental optima into actionable engineering decisions.

1 · Economic optimum

The rapid economic model evaluates feasible commercial NPS / schedule candidates and identifies the minimum screened transport cost. The ECOB correlation is retained as a rapid target predictor and diagnostic.

2 · Climate optimum

The streamlined LCA evaluates the same feasible commercial candidates and identifies the minimum life-cycle GHG. The provisional LCOB correlation is retained as a target predictor to be recalibrated after the full sensitivity campaign.

3 · Near-optimal compromise

Among commercial sizes within the allowed cost penalty, choose the design with the lowest life-cycle GHG. This avoids forcing a subjective weighted objective.

4 · Decision value

Report Δcost, ΔGHG, ID gap, break-even carbon price, and the carbon-price-adjusted optimum to support early project screening.
